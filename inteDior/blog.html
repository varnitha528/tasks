Your living room is the heart of your home — and brightening it up doesn't have to cost a fortune!

Posted at 13:01h in Tiles by Sara Marling

Start with light-coloured cushions to refresh your sofa in seconds. Swap out heavy curtains for sheer linen drapes that let natural light flood in. Add a round mirror above your console table to reflect light and make the space feel twice as big. A cozy jute rug underfoot brings warmth and texture without overwhelming the room. Finally, a single indoor plant in a beautiful pot ties everything together with a splash of life and colour. Small changes, big impact. That's the magic of thoughtful home styling!
