The Story of CHOCOLATE.COM

At Chocolate.com, we take chocolate to new heights. As long-time believers in the universal language of chocolate, we set out on a mission to curate the best variety out there. Our master chocolatiers transform the world's finest cocoa into exquisite creations, honoring timeless classics while fearlessly exploring bold new ideas. From sophisticated truffles to gummy bears, espresso beans, and unexpected creations, we craft each piece with intention, package it to perfection, and deliver it fresh to your door. Whether you're indulging in delicious solitude, sharing with someone special, or sending the perfect gift, our promise is for extraordinary bites to make each and every moment luxurious.

A note from
our family to yours.

In 2020, our family set out to create a movement around food. Building on a long history of snacktime lore and entrepreneurship, we stepped into our dream roles as (literal) tastemakers and lifelong foodies. We named it as we aimed it to be—Greatest of All Time (G.O.A.T.) Foods. Our first venture, Licorice.com, paved the path for our future family of ten brands, including Chocolate.com, Pretzels.com, Caramels.com, and, now, cashews.com.Each shop brings artisanally crafted, gourmet confections, snacks, and baked goods from our kitchen to your door. Over 1 million friends have tasted, gifted, and collected with us. Thanks for joining!
